# About Me

I'm an Electronics and Communication Engineering student who enjoys building hardware from scratch. Most of my work revolves around embedded systems, custom PCB design, FPGA development, and digital design. Over the past few years I've designed boards ranging from simple sensor interfaces to multi-layer wireless systems and FPGA development platforms.

Currently I'm exploring Analog IC Design while continuing to build embedded hardware and digital systems.
